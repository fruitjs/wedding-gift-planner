Dowry is a criminal offence in India under the Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961.

Giving, taking or demanding dowry is punishable. Gifts given freely, without any demand, are allowed when they are recorded in a list signed by the bride and groom (Section 3(2) and the 1985 List of Presents Rules). If anyone demands money or gifts, call the Women Helpline 181 or emergency 112.
